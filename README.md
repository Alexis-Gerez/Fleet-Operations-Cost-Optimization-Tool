Fleet Operations & Cost Optimization Tool
Overview

Fleet Operations & Cost Optimization Tool is an operational analysis and decision-support application designed for industrial and automotive fleets.
The tool focuses on fleet management, preventive maintenance planning, and cost optimization, combining structured data analysis with practical operational logic.

This project was developed as a professional case study to demonstrate the ability to design internal operational tools tailored to real industrial environments.

Purpose

Fleet operations often rely on fragmented spreadsheets and reactive maintenance strategies.
This tool provides a centralized, visual, and data-driven approach to:

Organize fleet assets by type and usage

Analyze operating costs and maintenance impact

Support preventive maintenance planning

Improve operational efficiency and cost control

The objective is not to replace enterprise systems, but to show how custom operational tools can be designed and adapted to specific organizational contexts.

Key Capabilities

Fleet Management

Registration and classification of vehicles (light, heavy, motorcycles)

Key attributes such as model, engine type, load capacity, and usage profile

Operational Cost Analysis

Fuel consumption tracking

Maintenance cost overview

Tire and consumable impact analysis

Preventive Maintenance Planning

Maintenance intervals based on usage and distance

Visualization of upcoming maintenance needs

Identification of risk areas due to deferred maintenance

Data-Driven Insights

KPI visualization for operational decision-making

Comparative analysis across fleet segments

Exportable data for reporting and further analysis

Application Scope

This tool is intended for:

Automotive workshops and service providers

Industrial fleets

Logistics and transportation operations

Technical training and operational analysis use cases

It is designed as an operational support and training tool, not as a certified compliance or regulatory system.

Design Philosophy

The application was designed following these principles:

Practical operational focus

Clear and readable visual structure

Adaptability to different fleet sizes

Emphasis on decision support rather than automation

The project reflects an understanding of industrial operations, maintenance workflows, and cost control, supported by data analysis and visualization.
